The pump

Schwing SP1400 line pump, on hire

A stationary pump takes concrete straight from the transit mixer and pushes it through a pipeline to the slab, column or footing, including the spots a chute or a crane bucket cannot reach.

  1. Set upThe pump is parked where the transit mixer can back up to it, and the pipeline is laid to the pour point.
  2. FeedThe mixer discharges ready-mix concrete into the pump hopper, as in this video.
  3. PumpConcrete travels through the pipeline and is placed directly where it is needed.
  • Reliable performance
  • Cost effective
  • Faster construction
  • On-site support
